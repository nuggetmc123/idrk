Rago Extract Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Rago Extract is a VR extraction shooter. This page explains what information the game uses, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Rago Extract is run by an independent developer ("we"). You can reach us by messaging us in the official Rago Extract Discord server, or through the contact details on Rago Extract's Meta Horizon Store page.

The short version

  • We only use what the game needs to work: your in-game callsign, a random player ID, a device ID, and what you do in multiplayer (voice chat, movement, trades).
  • Voice chat and your movements are passed live to the other players in your game. We don't record or store voice chat.
  • We don't show ads, don't sell your data, and don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Purchases go through Meta (or Steam on PC). We never see your payment details.

Stored only on your headset or PC

Your progress (stash, money, level, gear, bunker), your settings, and any videos you record with the in-game Camera are saved on your own device. Videos go to your device's Movies or Videos folder and are never uploaded by us.

Who we share it with

  • Other players: your callsign, voice (when you use voice chat), your movements, and your shop listings are visible to the players you play or trade with.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (in the EU) hosts our game server. It runs the server for us and doesn't use your data for anything else.
  • Meta (and Valve / Steam on PC) handle purchases and your platform account under their own privacy policies.
  • The law: we'd only share information if we're legally required to, and only the minimum needed.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Children

Rago Extract is a shooter with realistic weapons and is meant for teens and adults. It isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Meta tells us a player's age group, and younger players get a restricted experience (for example, no voice chat with strangers). If you think a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we'll delete their information.

Your choices and rights

  • Voice chat is optional. You can turn your microphone off in the game or in your headset's settings.
  • Access or delete your data: contact us by messaging us in the official Rago Extract Discord server, or through the contact details on Rago Extract's Meta Horizon Store page with your callsign, and we'll send you what we hold or delete it (ban records may be kept to stop the same account being used again).
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have extra rights, like correcting your data or objecting to its use. Contact us and we'll help.

Security

Our server keeps as little information as it can. The owner panel is protected by a password, and only the owner and approved moderators can take action on accounts.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's patch notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: by messaging us in the official Rago Extract Discord server, or through the contact details on Rago Extract's Meta Horizon Store page.
