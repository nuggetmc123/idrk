# BRAWLBOUND — Rules & Mod Guide

This file has two parts:

1. **Server rules**: short, for players. Paste them into `#rules`.
2. **Mod guide**: for staff only. It covers how to handle slurs, how to punish, and how to ban.

Both are written in Discord markdown, so you can paste them straight into Discord.

---

# Part 1 — Server Rules (post in #rules)

**1. Be respectful.** No harassment, bullying, or picking on people. Trash talk about the game is fine. Going after the person is not.

**2. No slurs or hate speech.** No racial, homophobic, transphobic, or other slurs. No hate toward anyone for their race, religion, gender, sexuality, or disability. This applies to messages, usernames, nicknames, profile pictures, statuses, and your in-game name. **Using a slur can get you banned the first time.**

**3. No NSFW content.** No sexual, gory, or shock content anywhere.

**4. Keep personal info private.** Never post someone's real name, address, photos, school, or anything else about their real life. **This is an instant permanent ban.**

**5. No threats.** That includes "jokes" about hurting someone, swatting, or DDoSing.

**6. No spam.** No message floods, mass pings, copypastas, or random links.

**7. No advertising.** Don't post other servers, games, or channels without staff permission. That includes DMing members.

**8. No cheating or exploit sharing.** If you find a bug or exploit, report it to staff privately. Don't spread it.

**9. Use your real account.** No alts to get around a punishment, and no pretending to be staff or another player.

**10. Listen to staff.** If a mod asks you to stop, stop. If you think a call was unfair, appeal it (see below). Don't argue in public channels.

**Appeals:** DM a mod or use `#appeals` if the server has one. Keep it calm and explain what happened. Temp punishments can be appealed right away. Permanent bans can be appealed once, at least 30 days after the ban.

*You must be 13 or older to use Discord. Following Discord's Terms of Service is also a rule here.*

# Part 2 — Mod Guide (staff only)

## How mods should act

- **Be fair and consistent.** Give the same punishment for the same offense, whether or not you like the person.
- **Stay calm.** Never argue, insult, or trash-talk while you're moderating.
- **Don't moderate your own fights.** If you're involved in a situation, hand it to another mod.
- **Get proof before you act.** Take a screenshot before you delete anything.
- **Log everything** in `#mod-log` using the template at the bottom of this guide.
- **Don't abuse your powers.** No banning as a joke, no "for fun" timeouts, and no leaking mod-chat screenshots. Doing any of these loses you the role.
- **Don't go above your role.** Only Admins/Owner can give permanent bans, except in the instant-ban cases listed below.

## The punishment ladder

For most rule breaks, go up one step each time the same person does it again:

| Step | Action | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Verbal warning** | Ask them to stop in chat or DM. Log it. |
| 2 | **Official warning** | Use the bot's `/warn` so it's on record. |
| 3 | **Timeout, 1 hour** | Discord's built-in timeout. |
| 4 | **Timeout, 24 hours** | |
| 5 | **Timeout, 7 days** | Tell them this is their final warning. |
| 6 | **Ban** | Ask an Admin to sign off. |

You can skip steps if the offense is serious. Some offenses skip straight to a ban (see below).

## Slurs: what to do

Slurs are the most common serious problem, so this section spells out exactly what to do.

### In Discord

1. **Screenshot the message.** Make sure the username and user ID are visible: right-click the user and choose *Copy User ID*.
2. **Delete the message.**
3. **Punish based on how it was used:**

| Situation | Punishment |
| --- | --- |
| Slur **aimed at a person** ("you're a ___") | **Ban.** No warning needed. |
| Slur used **to be hateful** toward a group | **Ban.** |
| Slur used **casually**, first time ("lol ___"), not aimed at anyone | **7-day timeout** plus a final warning. |
| **Second slur** of any kind | **Ban.** |
| Slur in a **username, nickname, avatar, or status** | Change their nickname and give a 24h timeout. If they refuse to change it, or change it back, **ban**. |
| Slur **"censored" or misspelled** to get past filters (n1gg-, f@g, etc.) | Treat it exactly like the real word. Dodging the filter is not an excuse. |

4. **Log it** in `#mod-log`.
5. **Don't argue with them about it.** Say "Slurs aren't allowed here" once and take the action.

### In-game (BRAWLBOUND)

**Know what the game can and can't do right now:**

- There is **no in-game chat**. A slur can only show up in a **player name**.
- Names are **filtered automatically** on the player's side and on the server, so most slurs get replaced before anyone sees them.
- There are **no in-game kick or ban tools**. Mods can't remove someone from a lobby or ban them in-game. **Discord is where punishments happen.**

**If someone gets an offensive name past the filter:**

1. **Screenshot it.** Show the name and the lobby code if you can.
2. **Leave the lobby** and tell any players with you to leave too.
3. **Post it in `#mod-reports`** with the screenshot and the exact spelling of the name. A dev can add that spelling to the filter.
4. **If you know who it is on Discord**, punish them there using the slur table above. Making a hateful in-game name counts the same as typing a slur in Discord.

**If a player reports someone else:** thank them and ask for a screenshot. Never punish anyone based only on "trust me".

## Instant bans (no warnings)

Any mod can ban for these right away. Then tell an Admin.

- Slurs aimed at a person, or hate speech
- **Doxxing**: posting anyone's personal info
- Real threats of violence, swatting, or DDoS
- Posting gore or sexual content, especially anything involving minors (also report it to Discord, see below)
- Raiding, or bot or spam accounts
- Scam or phishing links (free Nitro, fake giveaways, account stealers)
- **Ban evasion**: an alt of someone who's banned. Ban the alt too.

## Other offenses

| Offense | First time | Repeat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spam or mass pings | Delete it, 1h timeout | Climb the ladder |
| Mild swearing aimed at someone | Verbal warning | Climb the ladder |
| Harassment or bullying | 24h timeout | 7d timeout, then ban |
| NSFW (mild, like a suggestive joke) | Delete it, warn | 24h timeout |
| Advertising | Delete it, warn | 24h timeout, then ban |
| Sharing an exploit or cheat publicly | Delete it, 24h timeout | Ban |
| Impersonating staff or another player | Make them change their name, 24h timeout | Ban |
| Arguing with mods in public | Ask them to take it to DMs or appeals | 1h timeout |

## When to escalate (don't handle these alone)

Tell an **Admin/Owner right away**, and report to **Discord Trust & Safety** (https://dis.gd/report), if someone:

- Says they're going to hurt themselves. Be kind, point them to a crisis line, and get an Admin.
- Threatens to really hurt someone
- Shares anything sexual involving a minor. **Don't screenshot or share it.** Report it to Discord and ban the user.
- Seems to be an adult trying to groom a younger member

## Mod-log template

Copy this into `#mod-log` every time you take an action:

```
User: @name (ID: 123456789)
Action: warn / timeout 24h / ban
Rule broken: #2 slurs
What happened: called another player a slur in #general
Proof: [screenshot]
Mod: @you
```

## Appeals

- A **different mod** than the one who gave the punishment reviews the appeal.
- If they're honest about it and it was a first offense, you can lower the punishment. That's fine.
- **Slur, doxxing, and threat bans need an Admin to lift them.**
- If a ban is lifted, log it, and tell the user that any next offense is a permanent ban with no appeal.
