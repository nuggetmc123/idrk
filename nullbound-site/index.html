Built for real fights

Your hands are your fists. Every hit is physics.

Throw real punches

Jab, cross, hook and uppercut with your own arms. Block with your guard and counter when they open up.

47

Fighters to unlock

Every fighter has their own look and their own special. Earn coins and open crates to unlock them.

Physics ragdolls

Land a special and your opponent goes flying as a full physics ragdoll. Get hit yourself and you see it from inside your own ragdoll.

8

Players online

Quick match into rooms of up to 8 players, or make a private lobby with a code for your friends. Bots fill the empty spots.

Two places to fight

Train in the Combat Test Facility hall, then roam the open Battleground with its village, towers and cliffs.

Voice chat and moderation

Talk to other players with open mic or push-to-talk, and play in lobbies looked after by moderators.
