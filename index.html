BRAWLBOUND

Pick a fighter, drop into a barricaded room, and knock everyone else out of it for five minutes. Every time you are eliminated you come straight back to choose again.

How it works

Three opponents drop in with you and keep coming back for five minutes. Every elimination is 10 battle pass XP, and the match itself is worth 50 more.

Controls
Keyboard — move WASD, aim with the mouse, attack click or J, special F
Gamepad — left stick moves, right stick aims, RT attacks, LT special
Touch — drag the left side to move, the right side to aim and fire, tap SPECIAL for your ability
Esc pauses the match.

Play with friends

Five minutes against three opponents.
