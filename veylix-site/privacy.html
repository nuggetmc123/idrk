In the cockpit

Your hands on the wheel

Reach out and grip the rim to steer. The wheel has weight, gets heavier with speed and pulls back to centre when you let go. Kerbs, slides and impacts come through the controllers.

Your driver

Pick a name, a race number, and your suit, helmet design, visor and gloves. Your driver stands by your car in the garage and sits in it on track, and other players see them online.

The watch

Press the menu button and a screen rises from the watch on your left wrist: volumes, music, pit stop, reset, view and restart. Press again to put it away.

Racing rules

Track limits with warnings and time penalties, yellow flags around stopped cars, blue flags when you are about to be lapped, and pit stops for fresh tyres and repairs.

Crashes

Hit something hard and parts come off. Run sideways over a kerb or into the gravel too fast and the car can trip and roll.

Weather and time

Sunrise to night with stars, and clear skies to heavy rain. Rain soaks the track, which dries out again when the rain stops.

Comfort

Snap turning in the garage, a fade to the outside view in big crashes, and a recenter button. You can also play on a monitor with keyboard or gamepad.

Online

Race your friends

Make a party and share its six-digit code. The party leader picks the race and everyone goes together, either just the party or the party against other players. After the race you are all back in the party. Private rooms work the same way, with a code and no strangers.

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

In short: Veylix has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your progress and settings are saved on your device only. When you play online, the details other players need to race against you go through our multiplayer provider.

Saved on your device

The game keeps these on your headset or computer. They are not sent to us.

  • Your driver: the name and race number you choose, and your suit, helmet, visor and glove choices.
  • Your progress: lap times, personal bests, best-lap replays for time trials, championship standings and race statistics.
  • Your car setups and your settings, such as volumes, controls and graphics.
  • A local error log (Veylix-errors.txt), which records problems the game runs into so you can choose to send it to us if you ask for help. It stays on your device unless you send it yourself.

Online play

Online races, parties and Find Race use Photon, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games. When you play online, the game shares the following with the other players in your room, through Photon's servers:

  • your driver name and race number,
  • your car, its colours and livery, and your suit, helmet and glove choices,
  • your car's position, speed and steering while you race,
  • the room or party code you joined.

To connect you, Photon also receives technical information such as your IP address and region. Photon handles that information under its own privacy policy at photonengine.com/privacy. We do not store any of this ourselves. There is no text or voice chat in Veylix.

Your driver name is visible to everyone in the same race or party, so don't use your real full name or anything private as your driver name.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email address or password.
  • No payment information. The game has no in-app purchases.
  • No advertising, and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

The store you got Veylix from, and your headset's platform, may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Children

Veylix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Players must also meet the age requirements of the platform they play on.

Your choices

  • Change your driver name and kit at any time from the Driver screen in the game.
  • Skip online play and nothing about you leaves your device.
  • Delete everything the game has saved by uninstalling it or clearing its app data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Veylix handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
