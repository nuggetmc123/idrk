# Rago Extract

A VR ragdoll extraction shooter for Unity (Meta Quest + PC VR), in the spirit of Ghost of Tabor and Contractors: Exfil Zone.

Everything (guns, characters, maps, textures, sounds) is generated by code at runtime. There are no imported art files.

## Open it

1. Install **Unity 6000.0.40f1** with **Android Build Support** (+ OpenJDK + Android SDK & NDK) from Unity Hub.
2. Unzip `RagoExtract.zip` first (right-click → **Extract All**). Don't open it from inside the zip.
   Unity Hub → **Add → Add project from disk** → pick the folder that directly contains
   `Assets`, `Packages` and `ProjectSettings`. If Hub says *"not a valid project"*, you picked
   the folder one level too high (or the zip itself).
3. On first open the project sets itself up (Quest player settings, the game scene, the world in the Scene view) and opens the **Rago Extract** tab.
4. Press **Play** to try it. Without a headset you can play with keyboard + mouse.

## The Rago Extract tab (top menu → Rago Extract)

Three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set Up VR** | Switches on OpenXR for Quest (Android) and PC VR, with Meta Quest support and the Touch controller profiles. XR Plug-in Management and OpenXR are already in the project's package list, so Unity downloads them once while the project first opens and this button takes seconds |
| **Multiplayer** | Opens the Photon setup window: 1) get a free Photon App ID, 2) paste it, 3) import Photon Fusion 2, then **Finish setup now** |
| **Build APK** | Switches to Android and builds `Builds/RagoExtract.apk` (asks to set up VR first if you haven't) |

### Multiplayer (Photon Fusion 2)
1. Press **Open Photon website**, make an account, **Create a new app** → **Fusion** → **Fusion 2**, and copy the App ID.
2. Paste it in the window.
3. Press **Import Photon Fusion 2**. If the SDK is already in your Downloads it's imported straight away; otherwise the Photon download page opens and the SDK is imported automatically as soon as the download finishes (or use **Pick .unitypackage...**).
4. Press **Finish setup now**. Multiplayer turns on after one recompile.

The Multiplayer window shows a checklist ([done] / [ todo ]) of each step, so you can see exactly what's left. In the game, the raid terminal in your bunker says ONLINE when it worked, or tells you exactly what's missing.

Online play runs on Photon's cloud in Shared mode: everyone meets in the Market, raids are matched per map (4-10 players, aims for 8, launches at 1:00), and the session's master client runs the raiders (it hands over automatically if they leave). Voice chat is built in: your microphone is streamed over the same connection and others hear you from your position in 3D. Without an App ID the game runs offline (solo raids with AI).

## How the game works

- **Bunker** is your private base. Everything you leave in it is saved automatically.
  - **Main hall**: the raid terminal, a settings board and the lift to the Market.
  - **Armory** (through the north door): rifle rack, a pegboard and shelves for pistols, sights, mags, meds, loot and keys, a panel with your cash and ammo reserve, and a workbench.
  - **Armor Room** (through the east door): armor stands for helmets and vests, plus shelves for backpacks.
  - **Shooting range** (west tunnel) with steel targets.

  Each stash section has a NEXT PAGE button when it's full.
- **First launch:** after a short LOADING screen you're in a dark registration room. Type your **callsign** on the terminal's keyboard (3-14 letters/numbers; on desktop, look at the screen and type) and press **CONFIRM** twice. The second press locks it in. **Your callsign can never be changed.** Every operator also has a 4-digit tag (for example REX#4821) so no two players ever show the same name; if two players with the same name and tag meet online, one of them is given a new tag automatically. Your callsign is shown on the steel nameplate in your bunker.
- **Training:** your first visit to the bunker starts a short training. Glowing markers and a line of text at the bottom of your view walk you through it: draw, shoot, reload, open your backpack, put on armor, see your stash and find the raid terminal. Your progress is saved, and **SKIP TRAINING** on the nameplate ends it.
- **Getting to the Market**: take the lift. In your bunker it's on the south wall (behind you when you spawn, under LIFT - MARKET). Press **CALL** on the panel next to it: the bell rings and the doors slide open. Walk in, and the doors close behind you and the lift goes up (or press GO UP inside). You arrive inside the Market's lift, which opens onto the Market. To go back, use the lift on the Market's east wall (LIFT - BUNKER) the same way.
- **Market** is a shared safe zone where players meet, with proximity voice chat. It has 4 NPC traders:
  - **Viktor** (rifles and shotguns)
  - **Mara** (sidearms, SMGs, holo sights, ammo, basic backpacks; also buys armor and bags)
  - **Doc Hale** (meds; also buys valuables)
  - **Silas** the Fence (buys **anything**, pays the best price for valuables, sells vault keycards)

  Each trader has a chalkboard price list: pull the paper **buy** tag next to an item and it appears on the PICKUP tray. Put items on the SELL tray to sell them. A backpack sells together with everything inside it.
- **Armor**: you start with none on. It's only found in raids: armor cases (olive boxes with a yellow stripe), crates, Raiders (heavies always wear a helmet and vest, and drop them), and vaults (Epic and Vault tiers). New profiles get a bump helmet and soft vest on the stands in the Armor Room. If you die and own no armor at all, a fresh basic set is waiting there.

  | Tier | Helmet (head damage stopped) | Vest (chest damage stopped, stomach 60% of that) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Common | Bump Helmet 15% | Soft Armor Vest 15% |
  | Uncommon | Ballistic Helmet 28% | Plate Carrier 30% |
  | Rare | FAST Rail Helmet 38% | Heavy Plate Carrier 42% |
  | Epic | Assault Visor Helmet 50% | Assault Armor Rig 52% |
  | Vault (vaults only) | Juggernaut Helmet 62% | Juggernaut Armor 65% |

  To wear armor, hold it to your head (helmet) or chest (vest) and let go. To take it off, grab the top of your head or your collar. Armor you wear is lost if you die.
- **Backpacks** are soft military rucksacks (like Ghost of Tabor) that you can see on players' and raiders' backs. Grab a bag (or pull yours off your left shoulder) and the flap flips back onto one big open compartment. Drop things in where you want them and they stack up: meds, loot, helmets, vests, pistols and SMGs. Things a bit too big get squeezed in; if something doesn't fit, it falls out and you're told why. Long guns go on the **rifle straps on the sides** of the bag (let go of the rifle next to the side).

  | Bag | Space | Rifle straps | Where |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Daypack | 14 L | 1 | Mara, raids |
  | Assault Pack | 26 L | 1 | Mara, raids |
  | Raid Pack | 39 L | 2 | raids (an SMG fits standing up) |
  | Tactical Pack | 53 L | 2 | raids |
  | Expedition Pack | 78 L | 2 | raids (rare), vaults |
  | Mule Hauler | 126 L | 2 | vaults only (even fits a rifle inside) |

  Swap bags by putting a different one on your left shoulder. A bag keeps everything in it and on its straps wherever it goes (stash, floor, trader, your body).
- **Meds**: Bandage (stops bleeding), Painkillers (quick +15), IFAK (+40 and stops bleeding), Field Medkit (+60), Trauma Kit (+90 and stops bleeding) and Adrenal Stim.
- **Loot to sell**: everything can be sold, but some loot exists only to be sold, from junk to jackpot:
  - **Junk** ($25-$300): tin can, scrap metal, cigarettes, Zippo lighter, battery pack.
  - **Decent** ($650-$2,200): whiskey, smartphone, silver ring, military radio, gold chain, luxury watch, encrypted drive.
  - **Good** ($4,500-$6,500): intel folder, graphics card, rugged laptop.
  - **Jackpot** ($9,000-$45,000): cold wallet and gold idol, plus the vault-only gold bar, blue diamond and Crown Jewel.

  Pick something up and its name, rarity and value pop up.
- **Bruno the Foreman** (Market, right side) gives you **bunker expansion jobs**. Each job needs certain items plus a cash fee. Put the items on his TURN IN tray and press TURN IN ITEMS. When everything is in, press PAY & BUILD. The new room opens in your bunker; until then its doorway is a sealed, hazard-striped door.

  | # | Job | Bring | Fee | Unlocks |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Clear the Storage Room | 5 Scrap Metal, 3 Tin Cans, 2 Battery Packs | $4,000 | **Storage Room** (off the Armory, west): two more racks with shelves |
  | 2 | Build a Med Bay | 3 Bandages, 1 IFAK, 2 Whiskey Bottles | $10,000 | **Med Bay** (off the Armor Room, south): a med cabinet that restocks meds after every raid |
  | 3 | Wire up a Generator | 4 Battery Packs, 2 Military Radios, 1 Graphics Card | $22,000 | **Generator Room** (off the Armory, east): earns $1,500 every raid (holds up to $15,000); press COLLECT |
  | 4 | Dig a Trophy Vault | 1 Rugged Laptop, 2 Luxury Watches, 1 Gold Idol | $50,000 | **Trophy Vault** (off the Armory, north): more armor stands, gun racks and a trophy plinth |

  Turned-in items are saved, so you can bring them over several raids.
- **Party (play with friends)**: the PARTY board is on your bunker's east wall, next to the raid terminal. Press **CREATE PARTY** to get a 4-character code and tell it to your friends; they type it on the board's keypad and press **JOIN**. The board lists everyone in the party by callsign. Everyone stays in their own bunker. The **leader** picks a map and presses QUEUE on the raid terminal, and every member's terminal follows automatically: the whole party goes into the same raid (random players can still fill the free spots) and spawns together, side by side. The raid waits for the whole party, still launching at 1:00 at the latest. If the leader cancels, everyone's queue cancels. After a raid (or a trip to the Market) you rejoin your party automatically when you're back in the bunker. Parties need multiplayer set up (Rago Extract > Multiplayer).
- **Raid terminal** (in your bunker, east wall; there are no raid terminals in the Market): pick a map on the green screen and press the big **QUEUE** key. Each raid holds 4-10 players. The queue tries to reach 8, starts right away at 10, and once the queue timer hits **1 minute** it launches with whoever is queued. While you're queued, the top-left of your view shows **FINDING RAID** with a timer, then **RAID FOUND** and **JOINING RAID** (with its own timer, which runs until you are in the raid) when a match is ready.
- **Raids**: three maps.
  - **Dockyard**: container yard, cranes, warehouses, Harbor Office vault, and the **Workers' Village** through the west gate.
  - **Pinewood Outpost**: forest, military outpost, bunker vault, and the **North Hamlet** of wooden houses past the forest.
  - **Old Town**: streets, apartments, bank vault, and the **Suburbs** at the north end of the main street.

  **Every map is huge: 1 km x 1 km.** Around each town there's open country with roads out to far-off locations: farms (barn, farmhouse, silos), army camps (tents, sandbags, a watchtower, weapon/armor cases), gas stations, warehouses, cabin clusters and little streets. Each has loot and raiders. There are **3 extracts per map**: one near the town and two far out on opposite sides. A border fence surrounds it all.

  **Spawns:** you start out near the border, with every player in a raid at a different spot (10 per map, 6 on Dockyard because of the sea). About half are small lootable places: a tool shed, a hunter's cabin, a supply tent or an open shipping container, each with a few crates to search before you move out. The rest are out in the open behind a wrecked car, a rock pile or an old sandbag nest. No raider squad starts within 110 m of a spawn.

  **Field map:** press the **LEFT MENU** button (VR) or **M** (desktop). It's a real top-down photo of the map taken when the raid starts, printed on paper, with every extract circled in green and a red arrow showing where you are and which way you're facing. In VR it floats just above your left hand, turned toward your eyes.

  The village, hamlet and suburbs are streets of houses you can walk into and search room by room (stairs, furniture, loot crates inside), and each has its own extract.

  Each raid lasts 40 minutes. To leave, reach an extract (green smoke), step into the zone and use the **laptop** on the supply crate (grab it, click its CALL key, or press F on desktop). A **10-second** countdown runs on the laptop and your HUD. Stay in the zone until it hits zero; leaving the zone cancels the call. If you die, you lose what you brought and your body drops it for others to loot.
- **Raiders**: up to 16 per raid, spread over the whole map in small squads. They patrol slowly, moving to a new spot about every 30 seconds, notice you over time when they can see you, take cover, peek, fire bursts with human-like aim, flinch when hit, ragdoll on death and drop their gun and loot. A gunshot only brings raiders over if they're close (about 30 m); farther away they just stop and look toward the sound, and only come for you if they spot you. Footsteps are very quiet: walking, a raider only hears you if you're right next to them (about 2 m), sprinting about 5 m, and crouch-walking makes no sound. A vault alarm pulls in everyone nearby.
- **Woods**: pines with layered needle branches, leafy oaks and birches, bushes you can walk through and hide in, and patches of tall grass and ferns, all swaying in the wind. Raids have no draw-distance limit, only a light haze, so you can see across the whole map.
- **Guns**: 50 original guns (pistols, revolvers, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, DMRs, snipers, LMGs) in 5 rarity tiers.
  - Common ones turn up everywhere and Rare/Epic ones are hard to find.
  - 7 **Vault** guns spawn only inside vaults. Open a vault with a Vault Keycard, or grab the hack panel and hold on for 30 seconds while the alarm draws every Raider nearby.
- **Holo sights** (6 types) are found in optics boxes. Bring a sight to a gun's top rail and let go to mount it. The reticle is projected at infinity, like a real holo sight.
- **Effects**: muzzle flash with light, muzzle smoke, ejected brass, tracers, sparks, dust and debris, and bullet holes. Blood comes as spray, mist, wound decals, splatter on walls and floors, drips while bleeding, and pools under bodies.
- **Sound**: every sound is synthesized in code. This includes layered gunshots with echo tails, suppressed shots, reload mechanics, footsteps per surface, impacts, bullet cracks, alarms and ambience.

### VR controls
| | |
|---|---|
| Left stick | Move. Click to sprint (uses stamina) |
| A / X (on a hand without a gun) | Jump. With a gun in your right hand, use X. On the gun's hand, A/X still drops the mag |
| Left MENU button | Open / close the field map (in raids) |
| Right stick | Snap or smooth turn. Click to crouch toggle (real crouching also works) |
| Grip | Grab, two-hand a gun (grab the handguard), pull a slide or charging handle |
| Grip on a wall, ledge, ladder, fence or rock (empty hand) | Climb: the hand locks on and pulling down lifts you. Let go above a ledge to climb onto it |
| Grip at either hip or your chest (gun in the other hand) | Pull a fresh magazine for that gun. Put a mag back the same way to return its rounds |
| Trigger | Fire. Point at buttons and pull the trigger to click |
| A / X | Drop magazine |
| B / Y | Rack / release slide. Switches fire mode when a round is chambered |
| Chest | Grab a fresh magazine for the gun in your other hand (from your ammo reserve) |
| Right hip | Pistol holster |
| Right shoulder / lower back | Long guns |
| Left shoulder | Backpack: pull it off and it opens. Let go of things inside it to pack them, or next to its sides to strap a rifle on |
| Top of head / collar | Take off your helmet / vest. Hold armor to your head or chest and let go to wear it |
| Med near chest + trigger | Heal (bandage stops bleeding, medkit heals over time, stim gives instant HP + stamina) |

### Desktop controls (no headset)
WASD move · mouse look · Shift sprint · Space jump · M field map · C crouch · E grab what you look at / drop · LMB fire / click · RMB aim · R reload · F rack / open / interact (hold F on a vault hack panel) · X drop mag · 1/2/3 primary / secondary / pistol · Tab backpack · G drop (into the open backpack; rifles go on its side straps) · H wear held armor / take armor off

## Code layout
```
Assets/RagoExtract/
  Runtime/   core game (no package dependencies; always compiles)
    Art/     procedural mesh kit, materials/textures, gun, character and sight builders
    Audio/   sound synthesis + pooled 3D playback
    Fx/      muzzle flash, tracers, brass, impacts, decals, blood
    Items/   item database (50 guns, sights, meds, loot), guns, mags, containers
    Player/  XR input, physics hands, locomotion, ragdoll body, gear, vitals
    AI/      raid NPCs + squad director
    World/   level builder, raid maps, bunker, market, crates, vaults, extraction
    Hub/     kiosk / matchmaking UI, traders, stash, settings
    Net/     transport-agnostic replication (players, shots, hits, loot, NPCs)
  Fusion/    Photon Fusion 2 backend (compiles once Fusion is imported and multiplayer is switched on)
  Editor/    first-open setup + the Rago Extract tab (Set Up VR / Multiplayer / Build APK)
  Shaders/   lit (triplanar), unlit FX, holo reticle, sky
```
If the Photon module ever fails to compile against your Fusion version, the editor switches it off so the game keeps running offline; pressing **Finish setup now** tries again.
